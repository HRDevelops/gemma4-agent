We build on five lines of work. SWE-bench defined the task format; SWE-agent, OpenHands and Agentless defined agent designs. Test-time scaling papers show big gains from sampling, but they pick the patch with tests or a trained verifier, which we do not have. Agent fine-tuning papers use hundreds to thousands of tasks; we have under fifty. And evaluation papers argue for cost-aware comparisons and error bars, which we apply to a live leaderboard.
