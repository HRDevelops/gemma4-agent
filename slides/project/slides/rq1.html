First result: one evaluation is noisy. About a quarter of the tasks flip between seeds of the identical configuration. Modelling each task as a coin, one run has a 95 percent interval of about plus or minus five tasks, so even five seeds per arm cannot detect differences below four to five tasks. On the leaderboard, identical configurations scored anywhere from 0.08 to 0.20. That is why every comparison in this talk uses several seeds and paired tests.
