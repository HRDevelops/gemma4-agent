Research questions

Four questions, one per lever

RQ1 · Measurement

How noisy is one evaluation, and how many seeds are needed?

About ±5 tasks per run; use 3–12 seeds and paired tests.

RQ2 · Test-time compute

At equal measured cost: longer runs, or more runs plus selection?

Longer runs. Selection without tests does not generalise.

RQ3 · Weights

Does LoRA fine-tuning on solved trajectories help?

No. All three recipes reduced accuracy.

RQ4 · Agent design

Can trace analysis find a change that transfers to hidden tasks?

Yes: +1.6 tasks over Patchsmith, 0.17 → 0.20 on Kaggle.

Research questions
The project is organised around four research questions, one per lever a practitioner has. Measurement: how noisy is one evaluation. Test-time compute: longer runs or more runs. Weights: does LoRA help. And agent design: can trace analysis find a fix that transfers. I put the short answers on the slide; the rest of the talk shows the evidence.
